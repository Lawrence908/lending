Are Banks Tightening?

Lending standards are the earliest of the credit clocks: loan officers tighten before spreads blow out and, usually, before recessions arrive. Lending volumes are the opposite, trailing the cycle by quarters. This page shows both ends of the pipeline and computes both claims: the Fed's Senior Loan Officer Survey since 1990 with every tightening episode scored, and C&I loan contractions since 1948 with their lags measured. Nothing here is a forecast.

Sources and construction

United States

  • Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey via FRED: C&I standards for large/mid firms DRTSCILM and small firms DRTSCIS (quarterly since 1990-Q2), consumer willingness DRIWCIL (since 1982-Q2, sign inverted), C&I demand DRSDCILM (since 1991-Q4). The survey predates 1990; those older tabulations live in Fed publications, not FRED, and are deliberately not hand-transcribed in v1.
  • Volumes: H.8 bank C&I loans BUSLOANS and real estate loans REALLN (monthly since 1947), G.19 consumer credit TOTALSL (since 1943), weekly bank credit TOTBKCR (since 1973, in the payload). Year-over-year series computed here; levels ship alongside.
  • Outcomes: delinquency rates DRALACBS, DRBLACBS, DRCCLACBS, DRSFRMACBS and charge-offs CORALACBS, quarterly from 1985/1987/1991.
  • Recession bands: NBER dates via USREC, from the shared dataset.

Canada

  • Lenders: Bank of Canada Senior Loan Officer Survey via Valet, series SLOS_BUS_LEND with price and non-price splits, quarterly balance of opinion since 1999-Q2, positive = tightening.
  • Borrowers: Business Outlook Survey credit conditions balance via Valet (tightened minus eased), quarterly since 2000-Q3.
  • The Bank's historical E2 credit aggregates (1971-2020, terminated at the methodology handoff) were considered and skipped as peripheral to the standards question.
  • Recession bands: C.D. Howe Institute Business Cycle Council chronology, from the shared dataset.

Plumbing

  • FRED is fetched keyless-first (public CSV), keyed API as fallback; Valet has no keys. A failed fetch carries the stored series forward and says so.
  • Refreshes daily via cron: SLOOS lands quarterly on the Fed's schedule, H.8 on Fridays, G.19 monthly, delinquencies quarterly. Most runs verify rather than change; the stamp above says when data last moved.
  • Series follow the shared econ-core contract used across credit, housing, yield, jobs, debt and diesel, with a vintages field reserved for as-published views.
  • Raw data: /api/data, or the files under /data/.
  • Assembled with Claude (Anthropic). Survey balances, published aggregates, and computed history with both rules printed; no forecasts.
